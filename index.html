Hi, I am Dongli.

I am a 2024-cohort undergraduate student at Southern University of Science and Technology, working with ACTLab at SUSTech and preparing to visit the University of California, Berkeley.

Projects

Gentle Wanderer

Python · Humanoid locomotion · RL deployment

A quiet and stable walking pipeline for the Unitree G1 29-DoF humanoid, including reinforcement-learning tasks, reward design, selected policies, MuJoCo evidence, and deployment alignment for real-robot testing.

CV for Tennis Tracking

Python · OpenCV · YOLO · Homography · Guangdong Climbing Program

A computer-vision pipeline for tennis tracking and court localization, combining camera calibration, homography-based ground-coordinate mapping, YOLO/OpenCV detection, landmark maps, heatmap-based target selection, and full-court object retrieval. This work is connected to my 2025-2026 Guangdong Province "Climbing Program" participation.
